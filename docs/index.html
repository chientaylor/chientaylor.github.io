taylor@taypc6:~$ cat research.txt

A Comparison of Pre-Built Hypervisors
SUNY Poly · Independent Study · 2017

Benchmarked Proxmox VE, XenServer, Hyper-V, and VMware ESXi against a bare-metal baseline across 14 Dell servers. Proxmox came out ahead — top scores on over half the benchmarks, the most robust installer, and the richest feature set.

github.com/chientaylor/hypervisor-comparison-paper
Building an ARM-based Datacenter
SUNY Poly · Independent Study · 2017

Built a small datacenter from ~18 heterogeneous ARM single-board computers running LDAP, DNS, mail, and web apps, to see what enterprise services commodity ARM could deliver. Conclusion: the consumer ARM ecosystem was still too fractured to rely on — though a few boards (Odroid XU4, Udoo Quad) held up well throughout.

github.com/chientaylor/arm-datacenter-paper
